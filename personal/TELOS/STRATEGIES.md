# STRATEGIES.md

**Last Updated:** 2026-03-29
**Summary:** High-level approaches to achieving goals and overcoming recurring problems.

---

## AI Collaboration Strategy

- **Goal Orientation:** Focus the agent on the human goal (the "Why"), not just the technical step (the "What").
- **Scientific Loop:** Require agents to verify their work and analyze failures as learning signals.
- **Context Protection:** Load only what is needed to protect the context window budget and reduce token cost.
- **Checkpointing:** Frequent git commits to prevent data loss in long sessions.

## Professional Strategy

- **Fixed-Price Scoping:** Minimize "billable hour" friction by offering clear, value-based project quotes.
- **Maine-Local Focus:** Leverage local network and physical proximity as a differentiator for AI consulting.

## Infrastructure Strategy

- **Automated Nightlies:** Use Ansible Semaphore to handle routine maintenance, freeing up human cycles for creative work.
- **Self-Hosting (TrueNAS):** Maintain control over data and services while minimizing recurring SaaS costs.
