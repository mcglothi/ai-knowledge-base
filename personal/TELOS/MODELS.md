**Last Updated:** 2026-03-29
**Summary:** Mental models and frameworks used for understanding and problem-solving.

## Engineering Models

- **The Scientific Method:** Observe → Think → Plan → Build → Execute → Verify → Learn. (Meta-loop for all PAI tasks).
- **In-Place Editing:** Never append corrections below stale info; fix the source directly to maintain a high-signal memory.
- **Layered Retrieval:** Start with `_index.md` (fast orientation), then load specific domain subfiles (context efficiency).

## Business Models

- **ROI Framing:** Quantify the benefit of a technical change in dollars saved or hours gained.
- **Service Ladder:** Tiered consulting offerings from "Free/Low-cost audit" to "High-end implementation" to "Ongoing maintenance."

## System Models

- **TELOS:** 10-file framework for capturing deep context (Mission, Goals, Projects, etc.).
- **User/System Separation:** Customizations belong in USER/, core infrastructure in SYSTEM/ (portability & upgrade-safe).
