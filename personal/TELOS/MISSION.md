## The Core Mission

To leverage deep technical expertise in DevOps, cloud infrastructure, and automation to build systems that magnify human potential, starting with personal efficiency and high-value client outcomes.

## Key Principles

1. **Activate Creative Potential:** AI should be used to eliminate drudgery and allow for high-agency, creative work.
2. **Infrastructure for Everyone:** High-quality, agentic AI infrastructure shouldn't be reserved for the elite; it's the baseline for the future.
3. **Goal-Oriented Action:** Every technical choice should serve a clear, high-level goal (TELOS).
4. **Transparent & Auditable:** Systems should be well-documented (AIKB), versioned (Git), and easy to understand.
